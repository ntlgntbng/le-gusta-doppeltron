Outboard Processor & Upgraded Crossover Synergy

Woofer Tier 1 & Hop 09 Synergy

The upgraded Jantzen 14 AWG C-Coil Toroid (0.055Ω DCR) eliminates resistance bottlenecks found in stock iron coils (0.5Ω+). This allows the Crown PT-3 amp's >1000 Damping Factor to reach the 152WR woofer coils directly, executing instantaneous electromagnetic braking during 28Hz subharmonic hits from the dbx 120XP.

Midrange Tier 2 & Hop 03/07 Synergy

The 38.56µF multi-cap bank (Solen + Mundorf Classic) combined with the Mundorf CFC16 Copper Foil Inductor provides near-zero ESR and zero skin effect. This handles ultra-fast dynamic transient spikes expanded by the dbx 3BX and preserves microsecond timing corrections from the BBE 882i Maximizer.

Tweeter Tier 2 & Hop 06 Synergy

The Vishay 30W Non-Inductive Resistor L-pad attenuates horn sensitivity by 3dB with flat phase. It works in lockstep with the dbx 1066 PeakStopPlus limiter (+6 dBu) and internal Klixon/Bussmann protection loop to allow crystal-clear treble detail while preventing voice coil burnout during high-SPL runs.

LOOP

In-Series Thermal & Fuse Protection Loop

The main external Red Binding Post on the Figure 9 plate is disconnected from the main crossover feed. Instead, it jumps via a 12 AWG solid copper wire bridge straight to the Klixon LINE (Top Screw) terminal.

CRITICAL PATH: The LOAD (Bottom Screw) terminal routes the thermally-protected current to the panel-mount fuse holder containing a 3A Bussmann ceramic fast-blow fuse. Safeguards fragile phenolic horn tweeter diaphragms.
